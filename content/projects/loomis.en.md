Elixir is an application designed for clinics and hospitals performing surgical operations. It helps manage everyone involved in an operation: surgeons, anesthetists, nurses, caregivers, etc.

{{<lnbreak>}}

Surgical operations can thus be carefully planned and managed thanks to an interactive virtual room layout that faithfully represents the operating room and the various people involved in the operation.

The application brings together all the information related to an operation: staff, equipment, patient position, accessories on the patient, medical device settings, etc.

{{<lnbreak>}}

Commissioned through EPFL's Junior Enterprise, this project was carried out as a pair with another developer, also a student at HEIG-VD. I was in charge of the front-end part of the application.
