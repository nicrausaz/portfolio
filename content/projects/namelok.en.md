Namelok Farm Association is an association founded in August 2019 in the hills above Morges, Switzerland, with the following goals:

{{<lnbreak>}}

- Supporting schools by purchasing school supplies and furniture, setting up canteens, building dry toilets and meeting other needs

- Sponsoring children so that they can go to school

- Developing a project around artemisia, a medicinal plant that treats malaria

- Providing access to drinking water for every school and every family through water purifiers

- Any other project that supports establishing the association in Kenya as well as helping the population around Naikarra

{{<lnbreak>}}

Secondly, the Association aims, in Switzerland, France and Belgium, to raise awareness of Maasai culture, its traditions and its wisdom, to help people discover their way of life and, when needed, to stand up for them.

{{<lnbreak>}}

Namelok Farm Association is twinned with Namelok Camp Association. The latter was registered in Kenya in November 2019 and of course pursues the same goals.
