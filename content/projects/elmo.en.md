ElmO is a game based on the famous UNO card game, built as part of a project at HEIG-VD. The goal was to use a programming language with a paradigm different from the ones we had already worked with. We therefore chose Elm, a functional language designed to build reactive front-end web applications.

{{<lnbreak>}}

ElmO lets you play UNO online in multiplayer, and offers the possibility of playing against the computer. You can create and join private game rooms with other players and/or bots.
