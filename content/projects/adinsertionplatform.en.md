Ad Insertion Platform is a company specialized in inserting advertising into video streams.

{{<lnbreak>}}

I was commissioned twice to build the company's website: first in June 2020, then a second time in September 2021.

{{<lnbreak>}}

The first assignment consisted of turning the existing site into a static website that the client could edit directly. The site was built with the static site generator [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/).

{{<lnbreak>}}

The second assignment brought changes to the existing site and added new features: new content pages as well as a blog (news) and the company's job offers.
